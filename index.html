Abstract

We present a DiT-based latent diffusion system (up to ~180s, 44.1kHz stereo) with multi-granularity control spanning global, segment, and frame scales. Three components: (1) a multi-granularity conditioning system; (2) MusicRoPE, a hierarchical rotary position embedding; (3) SegAlign, segment-level representation supervision. The framework improves coherence by 11.9% and Beat F1 (0.28→0.77) over the text-only baseline, while enabling segment-level controllability (RMS ρ=0.705).
